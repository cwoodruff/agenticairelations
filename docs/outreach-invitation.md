# Draft invitation to critique

A draft for the author to adapt and send. Nothing has been sent to anyone.

## Who to ask

Aim for people likely to disagree, in roughly equal numbers:

- People who work on Agent Experience inside engineering, who may think this is not DevRel's job.
- DevRel leaders, who may think it over-claims or under-claims for the profession.
- People who run agent evaluations for a platform, who can judge the measurement specification.
- Open-source maintainers dealing with agent-written contributions, who can judge the commons rule.
- Analysts who cover developer tools.

## The message

Subject: Would you tell me where this is wrong?

Hi [name],

I've been working on a framework for what Developer Relations should do now that developers reach platforms through AI agents. I call it Agentic Developer Relations, and I've positioned it as the DevRel practice that works on Agent Experience.

The first version, from April, had real problems. I misquoted a source, got an Amdahl's Law figure wrong, and leaned on two studies that did not say what I claimed. I've corrected those and rewritten the framework. The review that led to the rewrite is public: [link to docs/agentic-devrel-critique.md].

I'd value your criticism of the new version, particularly [one specific question for this person, for example: whether the split of responsibilities between DevRel and engineering matches what you see / whether the measurement specification would survive contact with a real evaluation].

- The definition and principles: https://agenticairelations.com/what-is-agentic-relations/
- The measurement specification: [link to docs/measurement-specification.md]

Two things you should know. I do advisory work in this area and am building a related product, so I have an interest. And nobody outside has reviewed this yet, so you would be among the first.

If you're willing, I'd like to publish your response on the site with credit, alongside mine. If you'd rather it stayed private, that's fine too.

Thanks,
Chris
